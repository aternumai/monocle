# Code of Conduct

Monocle contributors are expected to keep the project respectful, practical, and safe.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent, but verify technical claims.
- Keep feedback specific to the work.
- Welcome new contributors.
- Treat safety concerns seriously.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks.
- Sharing private information without consent.
- Encouraging unsafe filesystem behavior that could harm users.
- Ignoring maintainers’ safety decisions after review.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject PRs, or block contributors who repeatedly violate these expectations.

For security-sensitive or private concerns, use the process in `SECURITY.md`.
